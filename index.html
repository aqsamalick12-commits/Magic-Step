Magic Steps

Climb seven levels of number theory, one right answer at a time.

📖 Based on Elementary Number Theory by David M. Burton, Units 1, 2 and 3

🔊 Sound ke liye screen par ek baar tap karein

📜 Game Rules

  1. 7 Levels hain, har level ke 3 stages: 🌱 Easy, 🔥 Medium, 💀 Hard.
  2. Har stage me 5 steps (questions) hain. Sahi jawab par aap ek step upar charhte hain, galat par phisal jate hain.
  3. Har question ke 4 options hain. Ek option par tap karein.
  4. Jawab sahi ho to "Good Job!" pop-up aata hai aur game khud agle question par chali jati hai. Galat ho to sahi jawab dikha kar khud next question aa jata hai. Next button dabane ki zarurat nahi.
  5. Question par tap/click karein to game question aur options parh kar sunati hai 🔊.
  6. Har stage me 2 lifelines (50:50) hain. Ye do galat options hata deti hai, yani sahi jawab ka chance 50%.
  7. Stage pass karne ke liye kam az kam 3 sahi jawab chahiye. Pass karne par agla stage/level khulta hai.
  8. Score: 5 sahi = ⭐⭐⭐, 4 = ⭐⭐, 3 = ⭐, us se kam = dobara try karein.
  9. Kisi bhi waqt Back button se pichli screen par ja sakte hain. Music aur awaz ON/OFF kar sakte hain.
  10. Har stage me kuch questions 📖 Theory ke hain jo seedha Burton ki book ke theorems aur definitions se liye gaye hain, baqi questions calculation ke hain.
  11. Topics: Induction, Binomial Theorem (Unit 1), Division Algorithm, GCD, LCM, Diophantine (Unit 2), Primes, Sieve, Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic, Goldbach (Unit 3).
